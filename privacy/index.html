Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Terms of Use

Who we are

Solfeggio (“the app”) is published by chocho apps. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad or Android.

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app has no sign-up and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share personal data.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you run a Voice Aura Scan or the Mantra Tuner. Audio is analyzed in real time in your device’s memory. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your sessions, streaks, challenges, scan results and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We have no server that receives this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). To validate purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally by your device. No push server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: https://chocho.io/
